Municipal results of the Dutch information security standards measurement, FAIRified

Per-host results of the half-yearly measurement of mandatory internet security standards by Forum Standaardisatie (edition begin 2026, reference date 31 December 2025), for the internet domains of Dutch municipalities: 26,064 test results for 1,867 hosts and 14 tests, described with W3C EARL, PROV-O and SKOS and linked to the municipalities (TOOI, Wikidata) and to the standards they check (NORA).
